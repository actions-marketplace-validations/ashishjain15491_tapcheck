# Code of conduct

tapcheck follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be
kind and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults, personal attacks,
and publishing someone's private information are not accepted, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else you
represent the project.

It applies to everyone who takes part: contributors, users and the maintainer.

## Reporting

If someone breaks it, tell the maintainer privately through
[a private report](https://github.com/ashishjain15491/tapcheck/security/advisories/new) and say it's about conduct.
Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential. The maintainer may warn, remove content, or ban someone
from the project, depending on what happened, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
